# GameGear Hub

🎮 **Find your edge. Build your PC.**

GameGear Hub is a public gaming hardware finder and custom PC setup builder. It helps players browse, compare, and plan a system around the games and performance they care about.

🌐 **Website:** [vflzx.github.io/ggpcs](https://vflzx.github.io/ggpcs/)

## What you can do

- Browse more than **75,000** built-in hardware listings across **36** categories.
- Filter and compare graphics cards, processors, monitors, peripherals, storage, and more.
- Build five complete PC recommendations from one budget. Use the slider at the top to compare balanced, value, graphics-first, processor-first, and high-frame-rate plans.
- Choose a part and let the builder rebalance the rest of the system around it.
- See prices, cited specifications, product imagery, and compatibility checks for each build.
- Save favorites and comparisons in your own browser.

## How the PC builder works

Enter your maximum budget, preferred resolution and frame rate, CPU and GPU brands, game, and upgrade priorities. The builder creates five different eight-part combinations, aims to use nearly all of your budget without going over, and avoids known conflicts such as mismatched CPU sockets, memory types, PCIe generations, insufficient power, and component clearance.

Catalog specifications are incomplete for some products. Any check that cannot be confirmed from the source is shown as **unknown**; unknown does not mean verified compatible.

## Catalog and prices

The built-in snapshot contains **75,777 product records**. **19,758** include a dated reference price. Prices are historical United States or France references converted to CHF using published European Central Bank rates; they are not current Swiss store prices or live stock availability. Product images load from their public source hosts when available.

Licenses, source versions, and price methodology are listed in [data/THIRD_PARTY_ATTRIBUTIONS.md](data/THIRD_PARTY_ATTRIBUTIONS.md).

## Privacy

Favorites, comparisons, and preferences are stored locally in the browser. The site does not have an account system or send this information to a server.

## Technology

Built with HTML, CSS, and browser-native JavaScript. The public site is hosted by GitHub Pages.
